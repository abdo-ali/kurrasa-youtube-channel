Kurrasa — channel management tool

Kurrasa is a YouTube channel of animated science, stories, letters and manners for children, in Egyptian Arabic.

This site is also the home page of the private tool the channel owner uses to manage the Kurrasa YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API: updating video titles and descriptions in Arabic and English, uploading subtitle files, and managing playlists and thumbnails. It is not offered to the public and only accesses the Kurrasa channel.
